TITLE: Direct evidence - animals - central injection - cognition and PFC
PURPOSE: Summarize central injection evidence separately from peripheral evidence.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: ICV/local brain injection in animals.
DEPENDENCIES: S5
KEY_QUESTIONS: What does central injection show about social memory, attention, and hippocampal-prefrontal function?
EVIDENCE_TIER: DIRECT_ANIMAL_PRIMARY
SOURCE_IDS_USED: S5
OPEN_ISSUES: Disease-model specificity and limited dose range.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

S5 SUMMARY:
- Species/model: Shank3-deficient rats (Het and KO), plus WT controls.
- Route: ICV into left lateral ventricle.
- Dose: 2 ng oxytocin before in vivo LTP induction; behavioral testing preceded by ICV oxytocin/vehicle (exact behavioral dose should be checked in full methods if further refinement needed); ex vivo slice experiments used 1 uM bath oxytocin.
- Outcomes: long-term social discrimination memory, 5-CSRT attention-like performance, hippocampal-prefrontal LTP.
- Direction: improved long-term social memory and attention deficits in mutant rats; restored hippocampal-prefrontal LTP.
- WT controls: no clear behavioral enhancement in WT animals.

INTERPRETATION:
- Stronger evidence for causally relevant central OT signaling on hippocampal-prefrontal circuit plasticity than for peripheral injection.
- Nonetheless, route is central and cannot be used as direct evidence for peripheral injected oxytocin.

VERDICT_FOR_STRATUM:
- Evidence level: MODERATE to STRONG within this narrow disease-model central-route stratum.
- Dose label: DOSE_ASSOCIATED_EFFECT; dose-response not established.
- Optimal dose: not established.

NEXT_ACTIONS:

- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
