KEY LIMITS:
- Peripheral peptide PK is short in blood and route-specific.
- Central exposure is tiny relative to peripheral dose and likely nonlinear by route/time.
- Behavioral effects may arise from small central exposure, peripheral afferent pathways, or endogenous release loops.
- Above-threshold doses may recruit off-target vasopressin signaling or produce countervailing physiological effects.
