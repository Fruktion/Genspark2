RED-TEAM SUMMARY:
- Human positive findings do not provide enough dose granularity.
- S4 supports only a narrow threshold-like pattern in one rat model and outcome set.
- S5 demonstrates efficacy at a central dose, not superiority over alternatives.
- Therefore any claim of "optimal injected oxytocin dose" would currently be overreach.
