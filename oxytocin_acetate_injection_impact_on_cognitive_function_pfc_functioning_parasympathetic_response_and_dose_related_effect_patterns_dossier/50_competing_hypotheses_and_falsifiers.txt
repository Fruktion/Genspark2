H1: Peripheral injected oxytocin broadly enhances cognition.
- Current status: weakly supported at best for narrow social-cognition context.
- Falsifier: multiple null broad-cognition injection studies with adequate dosing.

H2: Peripheral injected oxytocin improves PFC functioning.
- Current status: unsupported in opened human evidence; mixed/weak in animals.
- Falsifier: direct null neural studies after active peripheral doses.

H3: Injected oxytocin increases parasympathetic tone.
- Current status: weak and context-limited.
- Falsifier: rigorous HRV/RSA studies showing no vagal enhancement.

H4: There is an optimal injected dose.
- Current status: unsupported.
- Falsifier already partly present: S4 shows non-monotonic liability and no universal optimum.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
