KEY_QUESTIONS: Is any best dose supported within a stratum?

SOURCE_IDS_USED: S4; S5
OPEN_ISSUES: Human data insufficient.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

CURRENT_POSITION:
- No universal or even route-specific optimal injected oxytocin dose is established.
- Narrowest arguable statement: in male Lister-hooded rats under S4 conditions, 0.1 mg/kg s.c. is the best-supported tested dose for the specific outcomes examined because 0.03 mg/kg lacked key effect and 0.3 mg/kg caused adverse physiology.
- This is NOT the same as proving 0.1 mg/kg is the true optimum.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
