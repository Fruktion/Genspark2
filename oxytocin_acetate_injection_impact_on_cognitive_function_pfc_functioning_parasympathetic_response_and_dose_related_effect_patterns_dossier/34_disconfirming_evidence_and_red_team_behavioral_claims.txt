WHAT WOULD OVERTURN CURRENT_VIEW:
- Well-controlled peripheral injection studies showing no effect on matched social-cognition tasks across doses and populations.
- Studies demonstrating observed behavior changes disappear after controlling salience/arousal without cognitive mediation.
