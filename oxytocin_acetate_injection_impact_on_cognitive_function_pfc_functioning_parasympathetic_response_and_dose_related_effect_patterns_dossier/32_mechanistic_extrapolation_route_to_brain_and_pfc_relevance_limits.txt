TITLE: Mechanistic extrapolation - route to brain and PFC relevance limits
PURPOSE: Explicitly police what can and cannot be inferred from CSF penetration and central studies.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: Limits of extrapolation.
DEPENDENCIES: S2; S5
KEY_QUESTIONS: Why doesn't CSF access prove PFC-mediated cognitive benefit after peripheral injection?
EVIDENCE_TIER: MECHANISTIC_LIMITS
SOURCE_IDS_USED: S2; S5; S4
OPEN_ISSUES: Region-specific exposure data remain absent.

LIMITS:
- IV-to-CSF signal in macaques (S2) does not equal PFC receptor occupancy in humans.
- Central ICV efficacy in S5 does not prove peripheral efficacy because central route bypasses BBB and peripheral metabolism.
- Null PFC dopamine result in S4 weighs against overclaiming direct peripheral PFC activation from one behaviorally active s.c. dose.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
