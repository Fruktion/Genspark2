OBJECTIVE_FUNCTIONS_BY_DOMAIN:
- Cognition: improve task-specific performance without confounding sedation, hypothermia, or generalized arousal changes.
- PFC function: improve direct neural measures or circuit plasticity without overinterpreting task inference.
- Parasympathetic response: increase validated vagal indices or improve recovery while accounting for respiration, posture, and context.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Route non-equivalence.
- Species non-equivalence.
- Acute vs repeated exposure non-equivalence.
- BBB penetration and receptor cross-reactivity uncertainty.
- Obstetric/peripartum physiology differs from healthy adults.
