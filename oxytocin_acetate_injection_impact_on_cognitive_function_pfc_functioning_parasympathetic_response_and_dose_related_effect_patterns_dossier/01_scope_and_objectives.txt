PRIMARY_OBJECTIVE:
Build a dossier that answers only route-matched questions. Intranasal evidence is adjacent context, not direct support for injection claims.

DIRECT_EVIDENCE_REQUIREMENT:
A study counts as direct only if oxytocin was administered by injection/infusion (human IV/IM/SC or animal peripheral/central injection) and the outcome directly matches cognition, PFC function, or parasympathetic metrics.

TOP-LINE_SCOPE_LIMITS:
1. Formulation identity must be stated when known; otherwise label as generic oxytocin formulation unspecified.
2. Heart rate alone is not sufficient proof of parasympathetic activation.
3. Task performance is not direct PFC evidence unless paired with neural measures.
4. A single positive dose does not establish an optimal dose.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
