EVIDENCE_SUMMARY:
- S1: 15 adults with autism or Asperger's disorder received IV oxytocin and placebo in crossover fashion.
- Outcome: retention of affective speech comprehension after delay improved/maintained better in those receiving oxytocin first.
- Domain: social cognition only.
- Population: human clinical adults, not healthy general population.
- Route: IV.
- Directness: direct cognition outcome, but not broad cognition.
