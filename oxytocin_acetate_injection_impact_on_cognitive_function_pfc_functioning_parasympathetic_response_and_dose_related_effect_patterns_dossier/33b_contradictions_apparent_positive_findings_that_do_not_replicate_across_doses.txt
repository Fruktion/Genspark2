TITLE: Contradictions - apparent positive findings that do not replicate across doses
PURPOSE: Highlight narrow-dose positivity and adjacent-dose failure.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: Dose fragility.

KEY_QUESTIONS: Do positive findings survive adjacent-dose comparison?
EVIDENCE_TIER: DOSE_CRITIQUE
SOURCE_IDS_USED: S4
OPEN_ISSUES: More within-study multi-dose evidence needed.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

KEY CASE:
- S4 provides the cleanest opened dose-contrast: 0.03 mg/kg s.c. did not attenuate PCP hyperactivity; 0.1 mg/kg did; 0.3 mg/kg created hypothermia and some hypoactivity.
- This pattern directly argues against assuming monotonic benefit with higher doses.
- It also shows that a positive finding at one dose can fail at the adjacent lower dose and become physiologically problematic at the higher dose.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
