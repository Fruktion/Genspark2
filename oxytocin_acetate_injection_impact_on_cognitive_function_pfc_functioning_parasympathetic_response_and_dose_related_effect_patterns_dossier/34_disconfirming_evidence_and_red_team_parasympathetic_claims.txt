RISKS TO CURRENT_VIEW:
- Heart-rate slowing is not proof of vagal activation.
- Spectral HRV increases in pregnancy/labor may reflect mixed autonomic or hemodynamic effects.
- No opened human injection study here establishes resting RMSSD/RSA increase with explicit respiration control.

WHAT WOULD OVERTURN CURRENT_CAUTIOUS_VIEW:
- High-quality peripheral injection studies showing no increase in validated vagal metrics despite HR slowing.
- Or strong studies showing improvements only disappear once respiration/posture/task are controlled.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
