SCOPE: Direct injection evidence, dose patterns, and red-team challenges.
DEPENDENCIES: 01_scope_and_objectives.txt
KEY_QUESTIONS: What effects are direct, what are adjacent, and what would overturn current synthesis?

OPEN_ISSUES: Some questions remain unresolved because the literature appears sparse or formulation-unspecified.

PRIMARY_QUESTIONS:
1. Human peripheral injection: does oxytocin change cognition, PFC function, or parasympathetic metrics?
2. Animal peripheral injection: which doses/routes alter social cognition, attention-like behavior, learning/memory, or direct PFC-related measures?
3. Animal central injection: what direct PFC or cognition effects exist, and at what doses/timing?

SECONDARY_DOSE_QUESTIONS:
- What is the lowest reported effect-associated dose in each stratum?
- What is the highest no-effect dose in each stratum?
- Are there signs of threshold, ceiling, or inverted-U patterns?
- Do bolus/infusion differences matter?
- What evidence, if any, supports repeated-dose benefit or harm?

RED_TEAM_QUESTIONS:
- Are observed changes actually social salience/stress/arousal effects rather than cognition enhancement?
- Are PFC claims mostly inferential?
- Do parasympathetic claims survive respiration-control scrutiny?

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
