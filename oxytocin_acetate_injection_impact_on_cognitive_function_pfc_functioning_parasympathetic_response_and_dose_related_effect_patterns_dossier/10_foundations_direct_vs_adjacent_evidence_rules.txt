TITLE: Direct vs adjacent evidence rules
PURPOSE: Prevent route leakage and overclaiming.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: Evidence classification rules.
DEPENDENCIES: 10_foundations_compound_identity_formulation_and_route_scope.txt
KEY_QUESTIONS: What counts as direct support for the main question?
EVIDENCE_TIER: RULESET
SOURCE_IDS_USED: NONE_INFERENCE_ONLY
OPEN_ISSUES: None.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

DIRECT:
- Injected/infused oxytocin study with cognition, direct PFC, or parasympathetic outcomes in same experiment.

ADJACENT:
- Intranasal studies.
- Endogenous oxytocin correlations.
- PK/brain-access studies without target outcomes.
- Reviews and meta-analyses.
- Obstetric fetal-heart or uterine outcomes not directly relevant to cognition/PFC/parasympathetic outcomes.

RULE:
Adjacent evidence may explain plausibility or constraints, but cannot upgrade an unsupported injection claim to supported.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
