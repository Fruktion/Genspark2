LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Distinguish oxytocin acetate-specified evidence from unspecified generic formulations.
- Keep peripheral human injection evidence separate from animal central injection evidence.
- Distinguish social cognition effects from broad cognitive enhancement.
- Evaluate whether a parasympathetic claim uses appropriate metrics.

COMMON_MISCONCEPTIONS:
1. "Intranasal oxytocin literature proves injected oxytocin works." False; route mismatch.
2. "Any HRV increase means parasympathetic activation." Incomplete without method details and respiration context.
3. "A dose used in a positive study is the optimal dose." False unless superiority is directly demonstrated.
4. "If oxytocin changes behavior, it must act in PFC." False; may reflect broader salience/reward/arousal pathways.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
