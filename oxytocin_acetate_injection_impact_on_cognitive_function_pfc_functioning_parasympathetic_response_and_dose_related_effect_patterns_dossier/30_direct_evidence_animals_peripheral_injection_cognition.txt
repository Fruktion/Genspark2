S4 SUMMARY:
- Species/sex: adult male Lister-hooded rats.
- Formulation: oxytocin acetate from Bachem.
- Route: subcutaneous.
- Doses: 0.03, 0.1, 0.3 mg/kg s.c.; doses expressed as salt.
- Outcomes: social interaction, PCP-induced hyperactivity, 50 kHz USVs, locomotion/body temperature confound checks.

COGNITION_DOMAIN_MAPPING:
- Social cognition / social behavior: increased total social interaction and sniffing at 0.1 mg/kg.
- Attention/executive: no direct attention task in this study.
- Learning/memory: no direct learning or memory task.
- Decision/arousal: PCP hyperactivity attenuation at 0.1 mg/kg.

DOSE INTERPRETATION:
- 0.03 mg/kg: no detectable effect on PCP hyperactivity; no evidence of social benefit shown.
- 0.1 mg/kg: effect-associated dose for social interaction increase and PCP hyperactivity attenuation.
- 0.3 mg/kg: adverse physiology (hypothermia, some hypoactivity), not selected as beneficial social dose.
