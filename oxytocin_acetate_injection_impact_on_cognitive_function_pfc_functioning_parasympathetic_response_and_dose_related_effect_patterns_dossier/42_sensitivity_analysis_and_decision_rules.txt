UPGRADE RULES:
- Upgrade human peripheral cognition from WEAK to MODERATE only with at least one full-text direct study plus corroboration.
- Upgrade dose-response to SUPPORTED only with within-study multi-dose evidence or highly comparable replicated studies in same stratum.
- Upgrade optimal-dose claim only with direct superiority evidence, not just one positive tested dose.
