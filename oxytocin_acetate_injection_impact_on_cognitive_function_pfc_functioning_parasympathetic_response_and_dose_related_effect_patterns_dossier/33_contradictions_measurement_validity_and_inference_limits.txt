MEASUREMENT LIMITS:
- S1 measures social-affective speech retention, not global cognition.
- S3 reports spectral HRV changes and heart-rate slowing in pregnancy; this is not the same as robust proof of parasympathetic activation or recovery improvement.
- S4 social interaction is a behavioral outcome influenced by locomotion, temperature, salience, and reward; authors partially controlled these, but not all interpretive ambiguity disappears.
- S5 5-CSRT is PFC-inferred behavior, though the same paper strengthens inference with direct hippocampal-prefrontal electrophysiology.
