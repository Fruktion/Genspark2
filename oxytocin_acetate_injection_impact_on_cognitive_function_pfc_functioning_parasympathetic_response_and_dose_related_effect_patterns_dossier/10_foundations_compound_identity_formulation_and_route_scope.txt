FORMULATION_IDENTITY_NOTE:
- The opened literature does NOT show consistent specification of "oxytocin acetate injection" across human and animal studies.
- S4 explicitly states oxytocin acetate (Bachem), doses expressed as the salt, administered s.c. in rats.
- S1 and S3 refer generically to intravenous oxytocin without salt/formulation detail in the inspected abstracts.
- Obstetric oxytocin/Pitocin/Syntocinon-type preparations should not be silently equated with oxytocin acetate unless formulation documentation is explicit.
- Intranasal oxytocin studies are adjacent only and not direct support for injection claims.

ROUTE_SCOPE:
A. Peripheral systemic injection in humans: IV directly observed; IM/SC not yet directly evidenced in opened cognition/PFC/autonomic studies.
B. Peripheral systemic injection in animals: s.c. directly evidenced in opened full text; IP appears in searches but not yet opened as direct key evidence here.
C. Central injection in animals: ICV directly evidenced in opened full text.
D. Intranasal context: adjacent only.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
