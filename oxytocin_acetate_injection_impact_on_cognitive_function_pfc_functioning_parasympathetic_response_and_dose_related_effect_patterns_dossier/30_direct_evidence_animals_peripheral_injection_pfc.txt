TITLE: Direct evidence - animals - peripheral injection - PFC
PURPOSE: Summarize direct PFC measures after peripheral animal injection.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: Peripheral animal injection with direct PFC measures.
DEPENDENCIES: S4
KEY_QUESTIONS: Did peripheral injected oxytocin change direct PFC functioning?
EVIDENCE_TIER: DIRECT_ANIMAL_PRIMARY_LIMITED
SOURCE_IDS_USED: S4
OPEN_ISSUES: Only one opened study and it was largely negative for direct PFC neurochemistry.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

DIRECT_NEURAL_MEASURE_EVIDENCE:
- S4 measured dopamine and serotonin efflux in PFC and NAc by microdialysis after 0.1 mg/kg s.c. oxytocin acetate.
- Result: significant persistent increase in NAc dopamine; PFC dopamine trend did not reach significance; PFC and NAc serotonin unchanged.

INTERPRETATION:
- This is direct evidence AGAINST a simple claim that peripheral s.c. oxytocin at 0.1 mg/kg robustly increases PFC catecholaminergic signaling in this model.
- Behavioral changes in the same study should not be oversold as direct PFC enhancement.

VERDICT_FOR_STRATUM:
- Evidence level: WEAK but informative.
- Effect direction for direct PFC neurochemistry at tested dose: null/non-significant.
- Dose-response: insufficient; only one direct PFC dose examined in microdialysis arm.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
