PURPOSE: Evaluate whether higher-dose effects may reflect non-OXTR mechanisms.
STATUS: SUBSTANTIVE_DRAFT
SCOPE: V1a cross-reactivity, especially at higher peripheral doses.

KEY_QUESTIONS: Could some observed effects be vasopressin-receptor mediated?

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-27

S4 DIRECTLY SHOWS:
- 0.3 mg/kg s.c. oxytocin-induced hypothermia was attenuated by V1a antagonist SR49059.
- Same hypothermia was not attenuated by oxytocin antagonist L-368,899 under that protocol.

INTERPRETATION:
- At least some high-dose peripheral physiological effects are plausibly V1a-mediated or V1a-influenced.
- This directly weakens simplistic "more oxytocin = more beneficial oxytocin receptor effect" reasoning.
- High-dose adverse/countervailing effects can emerge before any universal cognitive optimum is demonstrated.

NEXT_ACTIONS:
- Continue targeted searches for route-matched full texts, especially human peripheral injection PFC and parasympathetic studies.
- Maintain separation between oxytocin acetate-specified studies and generic oxytocin formulations.
