RULES:
- Keep original units exactly as reported.
- Add conversions only as secondary information.
- Do not infer equivalence across IU, mcg, mg/kg, or ng ICV without explicit support.
- Distinguish total dose from infusion rate and from peak exposure timing.
- Distinguish dose associated with effect from proven optimal dose.

EXAMPLES_FROM_OPENED_SOURCES:

- S5: 2 ng ICV before in vivo LTP induction; 1 uM slice bath application for ex vivo LTP context.
- S2: 80 IU d5-oxytocin via IV and IN in rhesus macaques.
