COGNITION:
- Social-cognition tasks should not be generalized to intelligence, working memory, or global cognition.
- Disease-model rescue is not equivalent to enhancement in healthy subjects.

PFC:
- Task-based inference is weaker than direct electrophysiology or imaging.
- S5 is stronger because it pairs 5-CSRT with hippocampal-prefrontal LTP.
- S4 is valuable because it directly tested PFC monoamines and found no significant effect at the behaviorally active dose.

PARASYMPATHETIC:
- HR alone is insufficient.
- Spectral HRV variables require caution; respiration, posture, speaking, contraction/labor context, and nonlinear metrics all matter.
- Baseline tone, reactivity, and recovery should be separated; current opened evidence does not adequately cover all three.
