- OXT: oxytocin.
- Oxytocin acetate: salt/formulation explicitly named in some preclinical studies; should not be assumed when papers say only oxytocin.
- IV/IM/SC/IP/ICV: intravenous, intramuscular, subcutaneous, intraperitoneal, intracerebroventricular.
- HF-HRV/RMSSD/RSA: candidate vagal/parasympathetic metrics, but interpretation depends on protocol and respiration handling.
- PFC direct measures: fMRI activation/connectivity, electrophysiology, c-Fos, receptor expression, neurochemistry.
- PFC-inferred task: 5-CSRT, working memory, inhibitory control tasks suggesting but not proving PFC involvement.
- DOSE_ASSOCIATED_EFFECT: an effect observed at one or more doses without robust dose-response proof.
- OPTIMAL_DOSE_NOT_ESTABLISHED: default when positive evidence does not directly compare enough doses within same stratum.
