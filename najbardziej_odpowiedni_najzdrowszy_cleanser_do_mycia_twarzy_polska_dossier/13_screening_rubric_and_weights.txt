Hard gates for top shortlist eligibility:
- current INCI verified in >=2 sources
- >=1 source official or packaging-photo based
- availability in Poland confirmed in >=2 channels
- no unresolved INCI discrepancy affecting major interpretation

Scoring domains:
A) Barrier/tolerability 0-10
Signals favoring score: soap-free or likely syndet, milder surfactant blend, low fragrance burden, humectant/emollient support, scenario fit for sensitive skin.

B) Allergen/sensitizer risk 0-10
High score = lower expected risk. Penalize parfum, essential oils, high-risk fragrance allergens, MI/MCI if present, known ACD-associated surfactants/preservatives when applicable.

C) Cleansing adequacy 0-10
Need not be maximal; balance matters. Higher for oily/acne/SPF scenarios if formula plausibly removes sebum/SPF better.

D) Evidence confidence 0-10
Reflects literature support for the formula logic, not brand marketing.

E) Market verification 0-10
Reflects certainty of Polish formula/access snapshot.

F) Price/access 0-10
Reflects broad availability + reasonable cost per 100 ml.

Baseline weights to test:
A35/B20/C20/D10/E10/F5
Alternative reactive-skin weighting: A45/B25/C10/D10/E5/F5
Alternative oily-acne weighting: A25/B15/C35/D10/E10/F5
