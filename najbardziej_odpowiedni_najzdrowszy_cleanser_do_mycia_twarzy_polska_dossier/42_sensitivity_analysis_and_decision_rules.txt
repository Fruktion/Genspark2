SENSITIVITY FINDINGS
1. CLN-001 remains top under baseline and dry/reactive weighting.
Interpretation:
- Its recommendation is robust for users prioritizing barrier/tolerability.

2. CLN-007 overtakes the field under oily/acne weighting.
Interpretation:
- The dossier is genuinely scenario-sensitive, not merely unstable.

3. CLN-006 rises when barrier/tolerability is emphasized and price becomes relevant.
Interpretation:
- It is a legitimate no-regret option for reactive users, not just a niche outlier.

4. CLN-003 and CLN-005 improve when cleansing is down-weighted.
Interpretation:
- Their main handicap is cleansing adequacy and/or subgroup allergen caution, not necessarily poor barrier logic.

DECISION RULES
- If the user wears heavy sunscreen or makeup daily, do not default to the gentlest emulsion as a one-size-fits-all choice.
- If the user is on retinoids/BPO/isotretinoin or currently burning/tight after cleansing, shift toward dry/reactive weighting.
- If the user has known fragrance or EO issues, first filter to fragrance-free or no-fragrance-seen INCI products.
- If the user has known CAPB/coco-betaine or glucoside reactivity, apply a subgroup penalty rather than a universal ban.
- If pH is unknown, do not invent a preference; use surfactant system and trackable evidence instead.

ROBUST CONCLUSIONS
- Universal-winner claim is not robust.
- Scenario winners are more robust than overall winner claims.
- No-regret shortlist is more stable than exact 1-to-7 ordering.
