MAJOR WAYS THE CURRENT SYNTHESIS COULD FAIL
1. Reformulation / formula drift
- Because second-source INCI verification is incomplete, current product rankings could shift after reformulation detection.

2. Under-cleansing in real life
- The gentlest emulsions may leave residual sunscreen or makeup for some users, especially in single-cleanse routines.
- This directly threatens any universal-winner claim favoring the mildest cleanser.

3. Allergy subgroup mismatch
- Coco-betaine, CAPB-adjacent, and glucoside-family surfactants can still fail for some reactive users even in otherwise elegant formulas.

4. Market mismatch
- A product may be excellent scientifically but weakly available in Poland or inconsistently sold across channels.

5. Claim inflation
- “Hypoallergenic”, “for sensitive skin”, or “low pH” could be over-read if formula evidence is weak.

EVIDENCE FOUND THAT ACTUALLY CHALLENGES FAVORITES
- A06 challenges simplistic low-pH narratives.
- A10 challenges the idea that ultra-mild cleansers are always sufficient cleansers.
- A07/A08 challenge the assumption that rinse-off allergens barely matter.
- A04 explicitly documents small-study limitations in rosacea cleanser evidence.

RED-TEAM VERDICT
- The dossier is now meaningfully adversarial toward its own conclusions.
- A single universal winner is still not justified.
- The strongest defensible output is a scenario-specific ranking plus a narrow no-regret shortlist.
