Primary questions:
1. What cleanser properties are most consistently associated with lower irritation/barrier disruption in human dermatology literature?
2. How strongly does cleanser pH matter relative to surfactant system and overall formula?
3. Which surfactant families are generally milder vs harsher, and what are key exceptions?
4. Which allergens/sensitizers in rinse-off cleansers matter most in practice?
5. What do guidelines/reviews suggest for AD, rosacea, acne, sensitive skin, and dry skin?
6. When do very mild cleansers become underpowered for sunscreen/makeup removal?
7. Which products are actually sold in Poland right now, with current INCI verifiable in >=2 sources?
8. Among Polish-market products, which cleanser families best fit each scenario?
9. What evidence could overturn provisional favorites (reformulation, allergen findings, weak cleansing, etc.)?
