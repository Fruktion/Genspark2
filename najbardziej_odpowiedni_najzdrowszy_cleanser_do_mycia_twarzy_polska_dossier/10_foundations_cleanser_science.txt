Foundational synthesis:
1. Cleansing is necessary because water alone poorly removes many lipophilic impurities.
2. Excessively harsh cleansing can impair lipids/proteins and worsen dryness/irritation.
3. Mildness is usually achieved by formula design, not one magic ingredient.
4. Sensitive, atopic, and rosacea-prone skin particularly benefit from gentle, low-irritation systems.
