Working synthesis:
- Very mild lotion/emulsion cleansers may be ideal for barrier preservation yet weaker for resistant SPF or makeup.
- This does not make them inferior universally; it makes them scenario-dependent.
- For heavy SPF/light makeup users, a no-regret path may be either: (a) gentle cleanser used twice, or (b) compatible first cleanse + mild second cleanse.

Need stronger opened evidence directly comparing cleanser adequacy for sunscreen and makeup films.
