Normal/sensitive:
- prioritize low-irritation, not necessarily ultra-rich residue

Dry/dehydrated/barrier-impaired:
- creamy/emulsion/lotion cleansers with lower stripping tendency
- fragrance-free strongly preferred

Atopic/very reactive:
- mild syndet or non-soap cleanser, often fragrance-free, simple formula preferred

Oily/acne-prone:
- adequate cleansing becomes more important, but harshness can worsen irritation and treatment intolerance
- avoid assuming “strongly degreasing” = best

Rosacea-prone:
- avoid classic soaps, alcohol-heavy harsh systems, fragrance where possible
- gentle fingertip cleansing, no aggressive scrubbing
