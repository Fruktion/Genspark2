Simplified hierarchy (not absolute):
- Classic harsh reference: sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) high concern for irritation models.
- Often milder than SLS but still meaningfully cleansing: sodium laureth sulfate (SLES), depending on blend.
- Generally milder useful anionics: sodium cocoyl isethionate, sodium lauroyl sarcosinate, disodium laureth sulfosuccinate (context dependent).
- Amphoterics used to soften systems: cocamidopropyl betaine, coco-betaine, amphoacetates.
- Nonionics/glucosides can be mild, but “nonionic = always best” is too simplistic.

Important nuance:
Irritation is not determined by one INCI name alone. Whole blend, pH context, deposition/rinse behavior, and total formula design matter.
