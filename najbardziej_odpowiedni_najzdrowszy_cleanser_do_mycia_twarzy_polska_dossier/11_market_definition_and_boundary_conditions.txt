TITLE: Market definition and boundary conditions
PURPOSE: Define the Polish cleanser market universe and exclusion rules.
STATUS: DRAFT_IN_PROGRESS
SCOPE: Wash-off facial cleansers sold in Poland.
DEPENDENCIES: 01_scope_and_objectives.txt
KEY_QUESTIONS: What counts as in-scope? What is excluded?
EVIDENCE_TIER: METHOD
SOURCE_IDS_USED: NONE
OPEN_ISSUES: Boundary cases need product-level review.
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-03-31

In-scope product classes:
- gel cleansers
- emulsions
- creamy cleansers
- lotion/milk cleansers if intended for rinsing
- wash-off fluids marketed for face cleansing

Out of scope:
- leave-on micellar waters unless explicitly marketed as rinse-off wash step
- toners, essences, serums, masks, scrubs, peels
- prescription medicinal washes outside cosmetic cleanser framing
- body-only cleansers not intended for the face

Boundary condition:
Many brands position one product for both face and body. These remain eligible if face use is explicit and Polish availability is current.
