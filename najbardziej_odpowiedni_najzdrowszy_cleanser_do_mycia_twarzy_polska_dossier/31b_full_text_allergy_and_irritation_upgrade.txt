A06 pH study correction
ACCESS: ABSTRACT_ONLY / metadata only, but important corrective evidence.
RETAINED CLAIM
- Matching cleanser pH to skin pH does not guarantee mildness; surfactant system matters.
DOSSIER RULE
- pH is a secondary modifier after surfactant chemistry, not an independent proof of mildness.

ALLERGY RED-TEAM TAKEAWAY
- Fragrance-free is strongly favorable in reactive and allergy-suspect scenarios.
- Coco-betaine / CAPB / glucoside-family surfactants are not automatically disqualifying, but they justify subgroup caution.
- Preservative panic is not evidence-based in general; specific allergens matter more than vague “preservative-free” marketing.
