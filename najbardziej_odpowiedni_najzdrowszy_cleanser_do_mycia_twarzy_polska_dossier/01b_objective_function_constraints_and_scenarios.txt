Core score domains:
A) barrier/tolerability
B) allergen/sensitizer risk
C) cleansing adequacy
D) evidence confidence
E) market verification quality
F) price/access

Baseline weights to test, not assume as true:
- A 35%
- B 20%
- C 20%
- D 10%
- E 10%
- F 5%

Mandatory scenarios:
1) normal/sensitive without major disease
2) dry/dehydrated/barrier-impaired
3) atopic/very reactive
4) oily/acne-prone
5) rosacea-prone/erythematous
6) heavy SPF/light makeup user
7) suspected fragrance allergy/EO intolerance

Expected endpoint: scenario-specific winners + no-regret shortlist unless evidence unexpectedly supports one universal winner.
