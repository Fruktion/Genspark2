Evidence-based principles:
- Fragrance remains a recurrent contact-allergy signal, even in rinse-off products; risk is lower than leave-on but not zero.
- Essential oils and strongly fragrant botanical extracts should not receive a “natural therefore safer” pass.
- MI/MCI history matters; abstract-level evidence suggests rinse-off products with MI at meaningful levels can still trigger ACD in sensitized people.
- Preservatives in general should not be demonized wholesale; preservative absence can create microbiological tradeoffs.

Operational rule for shortlist:
For users with very reactive skin, atopy, rosacea, or suspected fragrance allergy, fragrance-free formulas get a major practical advantage.
