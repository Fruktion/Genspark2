High-level synthesis:
- Cleansing necessarily trades off impurity removal against some interaction with SC lipids/proteins.
- Soap/alkaline systems more often disrupt barrier, raise pH, increase dryness/tightness, and alter lipid/protein ultrastructure.
- Skin-acid-mantle reasoning is directionally useful, but not enough by itself; pH cannot rescue a harsh anionic-heavy formula.
- Residual irritancy and rinsability matter alongside surfactant chemistry.
- Mild cleansers often combine gentler surfactants with humectants/emollients and avoid highly irritating extras.

Implication for product ranking:
Products should not be rewarded just for “low pH” marketing. Whole-formula mildness is the relevant construct.
