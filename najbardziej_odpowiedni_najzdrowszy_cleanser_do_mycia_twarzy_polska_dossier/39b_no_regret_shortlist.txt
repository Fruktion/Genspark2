NO-REGRET SHORTLIST RULES USED
- Current official page opened
- No added fragrance seen or officially declared fragrance-free
- No major preservative scare ingredients evidenced in opened INCI
- Good performance across more than one weight set
- No severe traceability gap for basic formula identity

CURRENT NO-REGRET SHORTLIST
1. CLN-001 CeraVe Nawilżająca Emulsja do Mycia
Why no-regret: broad tolerability logic, fragrance-free claim, strong barrier-first formula, widely visible in Polish channels.
Main caveat: may underperform as a one-step cleanser for heavy sunscreen or makeup.

2. CLN-006 BasicLab Dermatologiczna emulsja myjąca do skóry ultrawrażliwej
Why no-regret: official Polish page with full INCI and price, fragrance-free, highly barrier-oriented, strong fit for ultrareactive and overtreated skin.
Main caveat: cleansing power may be modest; retailer diversity still under-documented.

3. CLN-002 CeraVe Oczyszczający Żel do Mycia
Why no-regret: fragrance-free official page, good compromise between tolerability and cleansing, especially useful for normal/oily users.
Main caveat: not the best pick for the most reactive or allergy-suspect subgroup.

4. CLN-004 Bioderma Sensibio Gel Moussant
Why no-regret: elegant mild-gel profile without fragrance in opened INCI, useful middle-ground option.
Main caveat: less ideal than creamy emulsions for very dry/barrier-damaged users.

OUTSIDE NO-REGRET FOR NOW
- CLN-003 LRP Toleriane: excellent candidate but coco-betaine lowers confidence for allergy-suspect subgroup.
- CLN-005 Avène Tolérance: promising but retailer/price verification weaker this round.
- CLN-007 BasicLab acne gel: useful scenario winner, but less universal than the four above.
- CLN-008 Cetaphil: excluded due to current Polish formula traceability gap.
