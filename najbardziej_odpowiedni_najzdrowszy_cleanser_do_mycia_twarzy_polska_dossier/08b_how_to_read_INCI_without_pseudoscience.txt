Rules of thumb:
- Read the formula as a system, not as one hero or villain ingredient.
- Presence of a surfactant name does not reveal concentration.
- “No fragrance” is usually more actionable for reactive skin than “contains botanical extracts”.
- Low pH claim is useful only if sourced and interpreted together with surfactant system.
- Preservatives are normal and often necessary; assess specific risk, not internet fear.
- Rinse-off exposure lowers risk relative to leave-on, but does not reduce it to zero.
