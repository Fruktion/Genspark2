REGULATORY TAKEAWAYS USED IN DOSSIER
- “Hypoallergenic” cannot be treated as proof of superiority without evidence.
- “Fragrance-free” is a concrete compositional fact if verified by INCI, but does not alone prove low irritation for every user.
- Marketing terms like “soothing,” “gentle,” or “for sensitive skin” are weaker than verified INCI plus barrier-oriented evidence.
