SOURCE-ID POLICY
- Axx = academic / guideline / clinical / mechanistic / review sources.
- Pxx = official brand or official product pages.
- Rxx = retailer / apteka / drugstore / marketplace product or category pages used only for market existence, size, and price checks.
- Gxx = official regulatory or government guidance.
- Xxx = inaccessible, paywalled, broken, or not-opened source metadata entries.

USAGE RULES
- Every product-level verdict must cite at least one Axx source plus at least one Pxx and one Rxx source when retailer confirmation exists.
- Search results and snippets can support market existence only; they cannot substantiate INCI chemistry or pH claims by themselves.
- Unknown pH remains UNKNOWN even if a product is marketed as “low pH” without an opened official source.
