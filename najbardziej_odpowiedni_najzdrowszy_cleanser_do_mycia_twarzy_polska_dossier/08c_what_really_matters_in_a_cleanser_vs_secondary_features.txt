High-signal features:
- whole surfactant system
- fragrance / essential oil burden
- scenario fit (dry/reactive vs oily/SPF-heavy)
- evidence and verification of current formula

Secondary features:
- color, marketing language, pharmacy prestige
- “natural” positioning
- one highlighted extract in a wash-off product
- foam amount by itself
