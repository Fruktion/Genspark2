Core logic:
- As barrier fragility rises, optimal choice shifts toward low-residue-risk, fragrance-free, low-strip emulsions/very mild syndets.
- As sebum/SPF burden rises, optimal choice shifts somewhat toward stronger cleansing adequacy.
- Therefore, “best” is not one point estimate; it is a Pareto frontier of cleanser families under different constraints.
