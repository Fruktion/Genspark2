Glossary:
- Stratum corneum: outer barrier layer of the epidermis.
- TEWL: transepidermal water loss; a marker related to barrier integrity.
- Syndet: synthetic detergent cleanser, usually milder than classic soap.
- Soap-free: usually means cleanser is not based on classic saponified soap salts; needs verification when used as a claim.
- Anionic surfactant: negatively charged cleanser agent; often strong cleansing, sometimes more irritation risk.
- Amphoteric surfactant: dual-charge surfactant often used to soften harsher systems.
- Nonionic surfactant: no charge; often milder but not automatically superior.
- Acid mantle: slightly acidic skin surface environment.
- ACD: allergic contact dermatitis.
- INCI: International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients; the ingredient list.
