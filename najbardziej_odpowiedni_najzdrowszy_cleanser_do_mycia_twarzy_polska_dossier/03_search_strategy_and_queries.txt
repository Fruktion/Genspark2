PASS_B_SPF_MAKEUP_REMOVAL
Goal:

Executed queries:

- makeup removal facial cleanser comparative study PubMed full text

PASS_D_REGULATORY_CONTEXT

SEARCH DISCIPLINE RULES ADDED DURING REPAIR
- Search snippets may confirm that a SKU exists in Poland, but cannot alone prove INCI, pH, or fragrance status.
- Unknown pH stays UNKNOWN.
- Official product pages outrank retailer pages for INCI; retailer pages are mainly for market existence and price.
- Broken / failed full-text attempts are logged under X-sources instead of being silently treated as opened.
