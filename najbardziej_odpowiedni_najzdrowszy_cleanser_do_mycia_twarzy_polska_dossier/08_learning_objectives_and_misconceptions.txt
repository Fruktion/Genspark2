Learning objectives:
- Understand why cleanser choice is a multi-objective optimization problem.
- Understand why pH matters but is not the whole story.
- Recognize why fragrance-free often matters more than “natural”.
- Recognize why product verification outranks brand reputation.

Misconceptions:
- “Low pH means automatically best.” False.
- “Apteka product means clinically superior.” False.
- “No preservatives means healthier.” False simplification.
- “Foaming always means harsh.” Overstated.
- “Natural extracts are always safer.” False.
