PROVISIONAL ROUND-END ANSWERS (SHORT FORM)
1) Is a single dex-to-cortisol blood-level conversion for PFC function justified?
- No. Current evidence weighs against it on endocrine, receptor, BBB, rhythm, and cognitive grounds.
2) Is cognitive improvement by glucocorticoid replacement/enhancement supported generally, only in special deficiency/clinical contexts, or not justified?
- Currently supported only in limited and context-specific ways, mostly as partial normalization in deficiency/replacement settings or as task/timing-specific effects—not as a general enhancement claim.
