LAST_UPDATED: 2026-04-01

COMPOUND IDENTITY
- Cortisol: principal endogenous glucocorticoid in humans.
- Hydrocortisone: pharmaceutical cortisol; generally used as the direct replacement analog in humans.
- Dexamethasone: synthetic glucocorticoid with high GR potency, negligible mineralocorticoid activity, strong HPA-suppressive action, and distinct PK/brain-access profile (S009, S023).
- Corticosterone: principal endogenous glucocorticoid in rodents; should not be conflated with human cortisol even though both are endogenous glucocorticoids in their respective species (S007).

USE-CASE SEPARATION IN THIS DOSSIER
- Dexamethasone literature is most relevant for HPA suppression, DST logic, and sparse direct cognition studies.
- Hydrocortisone/cortisol literature is most relevant for human intervention studies of cognition and for replacement studies.
- Corticosterone literature is most relevant for rodent adrenalectomy/replacement causality and stress models.

KEY NON-EQUIVALENCE NOTES
- Hydrocortisone is the closest direct pharmacologic stand-in for human cortisol, but even it does not recreate normal physiology automatically because timing/pulsatility matter (S022, S025).
- Dexamethasone is not a simple “stronger cortisol” for brain function because it differs in MR affinity, BBB transport, and feedback behavior (S023).
- Rodent corticosterone replacement restoring ADX-induced working-memory deficits is evidence about restoring endogenous rodent glucocorticoid context, not about human dexamethasone enhancement (S007).

WHAT THE OPENED SOURCES DIRECTLY SUPPORT
- Endotext provides clinical equivalence-table context and shows dexamethasone differs from cortisol/hydrocortisone in HPA suppression, mineralocorticoid activity, and half-life (S009).
- MR/GR review explicitly notes dexamethasone may function like “chemical adrenalectomy” for brain states because it suppresses endogenous corticosterone while poorly substituting in MR-rich brain compartments (S023).
- Human pulse-pattern work used hydrocortisone, not dexamethasone, underscoring that replacement physiology questions are usually asked with cortisol/hydrocortisone analogs (S022).
- Rat ADX rescue used corticosterone at physiological levels, not dexamethasone (S007).

DOSSIER GUARDRAIL
Never collapse the following distinctions:
1) dexamethasone vs cortisol/hydrocortisone
2) human cortisol vs rodent corticosterone
3) replacement/normalization vs enhancement
4) endocrine feedback suppression vs direct brain receptor engagement

PROVISIONAL TAKEAWAY
The compounds in this dossier participate in related but non-identical biological domains. Any argument that treats dexamethasone, hydrocortisone, cortisol, and corticosterone as interchangeable will overstate the evidence.
