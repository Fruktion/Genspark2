SEARCH PRINCIPLES
- Prefer PubMed/PMC and official NCBI or journal pages.
- Use reviews to orient but not to settle major claims.
- Log failed and thin searches honestly.
- Separate discovery from evidentiary status: found in search does not equal opened full text.

MANDATORY SEARCH CLUSTERS LOG

1) oral dexamethasone 0.5 mg cortisol suppression / dexamethasone suppression test interpretation
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed dexamethasone suppression test 0.5 mg overnight cortisol interpretation"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov oral dexamethasone 0.5 mg cortisol suppression dexamethasone suppression test interpretation cognition PFC"
Results opened/selected:
- NCBI Bookshelf DST overview
- PMC papers on DST interpretation and simultaneous dexamethasone/cortisol measurement
Why selected:
- Needed to test the common but likely invalid move from dexamethasone dose to single cortisol level.
What changed:
- Early evidence indicates DST logic concerns feedback suppression and test validity, not a direct cortisol-equivalent brain exposure metric.
Unresolved:
- Need direct low-dose DST papers with 0.5 mg regimens and interpretation caveats across assays and metabolism.

2) glucocorticoid equivalence dexamethasone hydrocortisone and limitations of equivalence tables
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed dexamethasone hydrocortisone glucocorticoid equivalence receptor occupancy review"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov glucocorticoid equivalence dexamethasone hydrocortisone limitations equivalence tables receptor occupancy"
Results opened/selected:
- Endotext glucocorticoid therapy/adrenal suppression chapter
- J Clin Pharmacol 2003 dose equivalency evaluation paper
Why selected:
- Needed explicit distinction among anti-inflammatory potency, HPA suppression, mineralocorticoid potency, half-life.
What changed:
- Evidence already shows equivalence tables are domain-specific and not automatically transferable to cognition/CNS effects.
Unresolved:
- Need more receptor-occupancy or CNS-focused sources explicitly criticizing overextension of equivalence tables.

3) dexamethasone GR vs MR affinity / receptor occupancy / BBB / CNS implications
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed dexamethasone mineralocorticoid receptor glucocorticoid receptor affinity blood brain barrier"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov dexamethasone GR MR affinity blood brain barrier CNS implications review"
Results opened/selected:
- de Kloet-related BBB/P-glycoprotein paper (PMID 9528963)
- Brain MR/GR review sources surfaced via PMC
Why selected:
- Central question requires knowing whether dexamethasone reaches the same brain compartments and receptor balance as endogenous cortisol/corticosterone.
What changed:
- Strong evidence emerged that dexamethasone is a poor MR substitute and brain penetration can be limited by P-gp, undermining simple serum-level equivalence for PFC function.
Unresolved:
- Need more direct human CNS or brain-receptor occupancy evidence, likely limited.

4) cortisol or hydrocortisone administration and working memory / executive function / PFC tasks in humans
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed hydrocortisone working memory executive function randomized healthy humans"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov hydrocortisone cortisol administration working memory executive function prefrontal cortex humans"
Results opened/selected:
- Henckens 2011 PNAS (10 mg hydrocortisone, timing-sensitive WM effects)
- Wingenfeld 2011 hydrocortisone impairment/null mixed study
- Porter 2002 elderly null study
- Young 1999 chronic hydrocortisone impairment study
- Schlosser 2013 response inhibition study in MDD + controls
Why selected:
- Direct human intervention evidence on working memory, inhibition, and executive function.
What changed:
- Human hydrocortisone evidence appears mixed and timing/task/context dependent, not uniformly enhancing.
Unresolved:
- Need classic dose-response human studies and more clear healthy-young versus older versus disease-state separation.

5) dexamethasone administration and cognition in humans
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed dexamethasone cognition healthy humans randomized working memory"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov dexamethasone administration cognition humans working memory executive function"
- "PubMed dexamethasone healthy volunteers cognition placebo cortisol suppression test executive"
Results opened/selected:
- Wingenfeld 2011 (4 mg dexamethasone in healthy volunteers; null on WM/flexibility)
- Additional search hits for declarative memory and PTSD-related dexamethasone studies not yet fully opened
Why selected:
- Needed direct evidence rather than inference from DST use.
What changed:
- Literature looks thinner than hydrocortisone literature; currently direct dexamethasone cognition evidence is sparse and often null or task-specific.
Unresolved:
- Need more opened primary dexamethasone human cognition papers, especially direct executive/PFC tasks.

6) adrenal insufficiency replacement and cognition in humans
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed adrenal insufficiency replacement cognition humans hydrocortisone cognitive function"
- "PubMed adrenal insufficiency hydrocortisone cognition study"
Results opened/selected:
- Harbeck 2009 nocturnal cortisol replacement pilot
- Buning 2015 randomized dose trial in secondary adrenal insufficiency
- Tiemensma 2016 mild deficits in PAI
- Schultebraucks 2015 subtle/no clinically relevant impairment in AI
- Krekeler 2021 dual-release hydrocortisone study
Why selected:
- Needed to distinguish normalization in deficient states from enhancement in healthy subjects.
What changed:
- Replacement-state literature suggests residual cognitive issues often persist; dose/circadian optimization does not consistently yield broad cognitive gains.
Unresolved:
- Need more direct comparisons of conventional versus circadian/dual-release regimens and whether any changes are true normalization or only subgroup effects.

7) adrenalectomy + corticosterone replacement + PFC or executive-like tasks in rodents
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed adrenalectomy corticosterone replacement working memory rat prefrontal"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov adrenalectomy corticosterone replacement working memory prefrontal cortex rats"
- "PubMed adrenalectomy corticosterone replacement set shifting reversal learning rat glucocorticoid"
Results opened/selected:
- Mizoguchi 2004 J Neurosci ADX + corticosterone replacement
- Mizoguchi 2008 hippocampal cholinergic follow-up
Why selected:
- This is the cleanest causal animal model of glucocorticoid deficiency and replacement.
What changed:
- Direct evidence supports normalization of ADX-induced PFC-like working memory impairment by physiological corticosterone replacement.
Unresolved:
- Need more animal work on whether replacement helps intact animals above baseline (currently doubtful) and more task diversity beyond T-maze WM.

8) endogenous glucocorticoids, stress, and PFC inverted-U or state-dependent models
Exact queries run:
- "PubMed stress cortisol prefrontal cortex inverted U review working memory"
- "site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov endogenous glucocorticoids stress prefrontal cortex inverted U working memory review"
Results opened/selected:
- Arnsten 2009 Nat Rev Neurosci
- Liston/Gould review on stress and executive function
- Shields 2016 meta-analysis on acute stress and executive functions
Why selected:
- Needed a mechanistic scaffold for why single conversions likely fail across state, timing, and co-transmitter conditions.
What changed:
- Strong support for nonlinear and multivariate PFC effects involving catecholamines plus glucocorticoids, not serum cortisol alone.
Unresolved:
- Need careful separation of endogenous stress states from exogenous steroid administration paradigms.

FAILED OR THIN SEARCHES WORTH LOGGING
- Some direct searches combining dexamethasone + healthy volunteers + executive/PFC yielded few directly relevant primary studies.
- Searches targeting exact 0.5 mg dexamethasone with cognition/PFC produced essentially no direct hits.
- This itself is informative: the premise may often be addressed indirectly through DST or equivalence tables rather than direct cognition studies.
