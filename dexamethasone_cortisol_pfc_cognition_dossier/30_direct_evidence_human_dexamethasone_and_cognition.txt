DIRECT STUDIES CURRENTLY EXTRACTED

Study: S001
Citation: Wingenfeld et al. 2011 Psychopharmacology
Species: Human
Sex: Healthy volunteers; sex mix not fully extracted from full text in current round, though study includes healthy volunteers and separate hydrocortisone comparison
Health state / model: Healthy volunteers
Endogenous glucocorticoid context: Intact HPA axis; dexamethasone used to suppress cortisol before testing
Intervention compound: Dexamethasone
Dose: 4 mg
Route: Not fully extracted from full text in current round; abstract implies treatment the day before testing in standard clinical/experimental administration context
Timing relative to testing: Day before neuropsychological testing
Acute vs chronic: Acute single-dose pretreatment
Comparator: Placebo and hydrocortisone condition in within-subject placebo-controlled design
Whether cortisol measured: Dexamethasone and ACTH/blood mentioned in MeSH/abstract context
Assay type: Not fully extracted; abstract indicates no clear cognitive effects despite endocrine manipulation
Cognitive task: Working memory and cognitive flexibility tests
Plausibly PFC-dependent: Yes
Direction of effect: No effect of dexamethasone treatment on working memory or reaction time in cognitive flexibility test; ACTH negatively correlated with cognitive flexibility in dexamethasone condition
Normalization / enhancement / impairment / mixed / null: Null at task-performance level, with endocrine-cognition correlation signal that does not rescue a direct cognitive effect claim
Major confounders: Abstract only; dexamethasone dose high relative to user's 0.5 mg question; healthy volunteers only; task breadth limited
Source-access grade: ABSTRACT_ONLY
Evidence-strength note: Important because it is direct dexamethasone cognition evidence in healthy humans and it is null, but current extraction remains limited by lack of full text.

WHAT IS NOT CURRENTLY SUPPORTED
- No direct evidence yet located that 0.5 mg oral dexamethasone maps to a specific beneficial PFC-relevant cognitive exposure.
- No direct evidence yet located that low-dose dexamethasone enhances executive function in healthy humans generally.
- No basis to infer from DST suppression alone that dexamethasone itself provides a meaningful cortisol-like brain state for PFC function.

SEARCH HONESTY NOTE
Current dexamethasone cognition literature is thinner than the hydrocortisone literature found in this round. Search results surface more declarative-memory, PTSD, oncology-survivor, or developmental exposure studies than clean healthy-volunteer executive/PFC trials.

PROVISIONAL INFERENCE (NOT A SOURCE)
At this stage, direct human dexamethasone evidence is too sparse to justify any confident pro-cognitive claim, and the best directly extracted healthy-volunteer study is null.
