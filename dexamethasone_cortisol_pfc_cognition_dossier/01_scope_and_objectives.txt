OBJECTIVE
Build a first substantive, reviewable dossier emphasizing upstream evidence over synthesis on the topic:
"Does oral dexamethasone 0.5 mg correspond to any meaningful cortisol exposure relevant to prefrontal-cortex (PFC) function, and can dexamethasone or physiological glucocorticoid replacement be used to improve cognition in animal experiments or humans?"

PRIMARY DELIVERABLE SHAPE
- A plaintext evidence dossier, not a polished article.
- Explicit separation of dexamethasone, cortisol/hydrocortisone, and rodent corticosterone.
- Explicit separation of equivalence domains: anti-inflammatory potency, HPA suppression, receptor occupancy, serum levels, brain exposure, and cognition/PFC outcome.
- Explicit separation of normalization in deficiency/clinical states versus enhancement above baseline in intact organisms.

NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARDRAILS
- Do NOT assume there is one valid conversion from 0.5 mg oral dexamethasone to one serum cortisol level for PFC function.
- Do NOT treat post-dexamethasone cortisol suppression as the cortisol-equivalent effect of dexamethasone.
- Do NOT provide actionable human enhancement advice, self-experimentation, optimization, or dosing recommendations for non-medical use.
- Scholarly description of study regimens is allowed only as evidence extraction.

THIS ROUND'S PRIORITY
Freeze most downstream synthesis polish. Prioritize:
1) source opening,
2) extraction from full text where possible,
3) contradiction logging,
4) non-equivalence mapping,
5) traceability by source ID,
6) provisional rather than overconfident conclusions.

EXPLICIT NON-GOALS
- Not writing a consumer explainer.
- Not generating a universal steroid conversion for cognition.
- Not collapsing PFC tasks, memory retrieval, consolidation, attention, and global cognition into one outcome.
- Not treating disease-model rescue as healthy-subject enhancement.
