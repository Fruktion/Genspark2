GRAPH (TEXT FORM)
A. Complete carbohydrate restriction + caloric deficit
-> ketosis / altered substrate oxidation [S001,S002]
-> fasting stress response / HPA activation [S006]
-> low leptin / altered thyroid signaling [S001,S008]
-> altered dopaminergic-lactotroph regulation (possible) [S003,S007]

B. Ketotic adaptation
-> increased PDK4 [S001]
-> reduced muscle glycolytic flux and postprandial lactate response [S001]
-> reduced GLUT4 / INSR / AMPK over time [S001]
-> worsened carbohydrate tolerance on refeeding [S001,S002]

C. Carbohydrate reintroduction
-> glucose rise + insulin/incretin response [S002,S004,S010]
-> rapid orexin suppression / altered central arousal [S010]
-> increased Trp/LNAA ratio -> serotonin/melatonin bias [S010]
-> possible delayed excessive insulin response / reactive decline [S004]
-> parasympathetic dominance / satiety signaling
-> postprandial inflammatory signaling in some contexts [S005]

D. Elevated fasting cortisol
-> increased baseline arousal and hepatic glucose support [S006]
-> sleep fragility and chronobiological disruption
-> carbohydrate ingestion may reduce relative stress drive or shift autonomic balance -> perceived sleepiness (inference)

E. Elevated prolactin / reduced dopaminergic tone
-> lower arousal, motivation, reward drive [S003]
-> metabolic dysregulation tendencies [S003]
-> may amplify postprandial sleepiness rather than uniquely trigger it

F. Fatty-acid profile
EPA/DHA -> suppress NF-kB/TLR4-type inflammatory signaling [S005,S011]
Oleic acid -> OEA/GPR119/GLP-1 adjacent mechanisms (requires more sourcing)
GLA -> DGLA/PGE1 historical endocrine/inflammatory hypotheses [S011 low confidence]

PRIMARY PATHWAY CURRENTLY FAVORED
A -> B -> C -> symptom
with D and E as upstream amplifiers and F as mixed modifiers.
