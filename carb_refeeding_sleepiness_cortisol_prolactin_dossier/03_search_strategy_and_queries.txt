SEARCH LOG
1. Query:
PubMed low carbohydrate diet muscle physiological insulin resistance GLUT4 refeeding cortisol prolactin fasting reactive hypoglycemia postprandial somnolence EPA DHA GLA oleic acid glucose metabolism review human studies 2026
Results opened:
- PMC article on ketogenic diet and glucose tolerance in healthy adults -> S001.
Why selected:
- Directly relevant human RCT with 12-week intervention and mechanistic tissue data.
What changed:
- Elevated confidence that low-carb adaptation can reduce glucose tolerance and alter muscle GLUT4/INSR/AMPK while sustained ketosis continues.
Unresolved:
- Need direct refeeding-duration studies beyond 12-week mixed-meal testing.

2. Query:
PubMed ketogenic diet refeeding glucose tolerance human study
Results opened:
- S001 (healthy adults, 12 weeks)
- S002 (weight-maintaining T2D ketogenic trial, 10 days)
Why selected:
- Gives direct human evidence on oral or mixed-meal glucose handling under ketogenic conditions.
What changed:
- Reinforced that weight loss is a major confounder; ketones alone do not guarantee improved glucose tolerance.
Unresolved:
- Need healthy humans undergoing abrupt carbohydrate reintroduction protocols.

3. Query:
PubMed fasting prolactin humans caloric restriction prolactin dopamine study
Results opened:
- S003 review on prolactin metabolism
- S007 abstract on fasting-induced PRL responsiveness
Why selected:
- Needed direct and adjacent evidence for prolactin-dopamine-arousal claims.
What changed:
- Lowered confidence that fasting universally raises prolactin in a simple way; the literature suggests altered dopaminergic control and context-dependent responses rather than one-direction baseline elevation.
Unresolved:
- Need more direct fasting serial-prolactin human measurements.

4. Query:
PubMed postprandial somnolence carbohydrate orexin serotonin reactive hypoglycemia human study
Results opened:
- S004 postprandial reactive hypoglycemia review
- S010 sleep/diet/glucose review
Why selected:
- Covers both reactive hypoglycemia and CNS sleepiness pathways.
What changed:
- Established that postprandial sleepiness cannot be reduced to only “reactive hypoglycemia”; orexin and tryptophan pathways matter.
Unresolved:
- Need direct studies correlating sleepiness ratings with glycemic trajectory in low-carb-adapted individuals.

5. Query:
PubMed gamma linolenic acid prostaglandin E1 prolactin review
Results opened:
- S011 historical/hypothesis-oriented GLA/PGE1 material via search outputs and related review-level evidence.
Why selected:
- Required by prompt; needed to assess Horrobin-style hypothesis.
What changed:
- Increased caution: many GLA-prolactin claims are historical, indirect, or contradictory.
Unresolved:
- Need stronger modern human endocrine studies.

6. Query:
PubMed EPA DHA insulin sensitivity cortisol inflammation human trial review
Results opened:
- S005 postprandial inflammation review
- S011 omega-3 cortisol RCT abstract
Why selected:
- Needed inflammatory and HPA modulation evidence.
What changed:
- Support for EPA/DHA reducing inflammatory signaling is stronger than support for large direct cortisol-lowering effects in general populations.
Unresolved:
- Need more dose-specific EPA/DHA HPA-axis trials.

7. Query:
PubMed low T3 syndrome low carbohydrate diet human trial review
Results opened:
- S008 eucaloric carbohydrate deprivation trial
- S009 low-carb/high-fat vs high-protein thyroid trial
Why selected:
- Needed thyroid adaptation mechanisms.
What changed:
- Elevated confidence that carbohydrate deprivation alone can lower T3, sometimes raise rT3, without requiring classic starvation.
Unresolved:
- Need data linking low-T3 adaptation directly to postprandial sleepiness after refeeding.

8. Query:
PubMed fasting cortisol carbohydrate restriction human study circadian meal timing cortisol

Why selected:

9. Query:

11. Pass label:
REFEEDING
Query:
ketogenic diet glucose tolerance GLUT4 human trial PMC11384946; ketogenic diet no change insulin sensitivity humans PMC11492932
Results opened:
- S001 ketogenic RCT full text (boundary reread for null findings)
- S002 weight-maintaining ketogenic trial full text (null/boundary)
What changed:

12. Pass label:
POSTPRANDIAL SOMNOLENCE
Query:
meal composition postprandial sleepiness 9284488 9426358; postprandial somnolence reactive hypoglycemia young adults PMC11651837
Results opened:
- S012 PMID 9284488 abstract/full PubMed page
- S013 PMID 9426358 abstract/full PubMed page
- S014 PMC11651837 full text case report
What changed:
- Added direct human evidence that subjective/objective post-meal sleepiness can occur without strong macronutrient differentiation in one design, while pure carbohydrate can increase subjective fatigue and slower reaction time in another.
- Added case-level evidence that OGTT-reproduced sleepiness can track delayed hyperinsulinemia and improve with antidiabetic medication.
Null/negative findings:
- S012 found no significant differences in sleep latency among high-fat, high-carbohydrate, or mixed meal constituents.

13. Pass label:
HPA-CORTISOL
Query:
circadian meal timing cortisol human trial PMC; fasting cortisol carbohydrate restriction meal timing human trial PMC
Results opened:
- S015 PMC5483233 full text
- S006 fasting/cortisol review retained as contextual support
What changed:

Null/negative findings:
- S015 found no significant effect of meal timing on cortisol phase, melatonin phase, subjective sleepiness, insulin rhythm, or triglyceride rhythm.

ROUND 2 MANDATORY PASSES
11. PASS NAME:
Refeeding and glucose tolerance
Queries run:
- ketogenic diet refeeding glucose tolerance GLUT4 PDK4 human trial PMC11384946
- low carbohydrate glucose tolerance refeeding human PMC11926029
- ketogenic diet no change glucose tolerance human study full text null
Opened full texts:
- S012 PMC11926029 acute 36 h crossover study of low-carbohydrate energy-balanced and energy-restricted diets.
- S013 PMC11743357 meta-analysis of overweight/obese T2D low-carbohydrate RCTs for contradiction and boundary-condition analysis.
Why selected:
- Needed direct human evidence on acute carbohydrate restriction effects plus a larger contradictory literature base showing settings where low-carb improves glycemia.
What changed:
- Added evidence that even one day of carbohydrate restriction can raise next-meal postprandial glucose and lower GLP-1 without changing insulin.
- Added contradiction that many T2D/obese low-carb trials improve HbA1c/FPG, showing population and weight-loss dependence.
Null/negative findings logged:
- S013: LDL and total cholesterol often unchanged; long-term superiority attenuates.
