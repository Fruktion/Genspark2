PHENOTYPE DEFINITION
The target state is not generic “post-lunch tiredness.” It is a narrower mechanistic phenotype with all of the following features:
1. Ongoing caloric deficit.
2. Complete or near-complete carbohydrate restriction long enough to induce fat-adapted metabolism.
3. Protein intake around 2 g/kg.
4. Fat intake enriched in EPA + DHA + GLA + oleic acid.
5. Elevated fasting cortisol.
6. Elevated fasting prolactin.
7. Excessive sleepiness emerging after carbohydrate reintroduction.

CORE CAUSAL QUESTION
Why would carbohydrate reintroduction produce disproportionate sleepiness in this specific endocrine and metabolic state?

FRAMEWORK
A. UPSTREAM STATE DRIVERS
- Carbohydrate restriction and fat adaptation.
- Energy deficit / fasting stress.
- HPA-axis activation and altered cortisol timing.
- Possible prolactin-dopamine dysregulation.
- Thyroid-energy adaptation and reduced carbohydrate oxidation readiness.

B. PROXIMAL MEAL-TRIGGERED MECHANISMS
- Transient impairment of glucose tolerance after low-carbohydrate adaptation.
- Larger postprandial glucose excursions and altered insulin/C-peptide handling.
- GLP-1 and incretin changes.
- Orexin suppression by glucose exposure.
- Tryptophan/LNAA shift favoring serotonin-related sleep-promoting signaling.
- Postprandial autonomic shift toward parasympathetic tone.

C. AMPLIFIERS / MODIFIERS
- Meal timing and circadian phase.
- Pre-existing sleep debt or hidden sleep pressure.
- Meal composition and dose of carbohydrate.
- Postprandial inflammatory signaling.
- Specific fatty-acid effects on inflammation, incretins, and possibly cortisol.
- Elevated prolactin lowering dopaminergic arousal reserve.

D. LOW-CONFIDENCE OR SPECULATIVE PATHWAYS
- Strong GLA -> PGE1 -> prolactin-normalization claims.
- Exact receptor-level membrane signaling claims not directly tested in the target phenotype.
- Strong histamine/GABA-centric explanations without direct source support.

EVIDENCE-GRADING RULES
Established:
- Direct human primary evidence or repeated human evidence with strong mechanistic convergence.
Probable contributor:
- Human evidence exists but is indirect, population-mismatched, or partially review-supported.
Speculative:
- Mechanistically plausible but weak, old, conflicting, or non-human dominant.

CURRENT WORKING MODEL
The leading explanation is a multi-causal sequence in which carbohydrate restriction plus caloric deficit induces a fuel-selection state that is relatively intolerant of abrupt carbohydrate reintroduction; carbohydrate then provokes exaggerated peripheral and central responses (higher glucose excursion, altered incretin profile, orexin suppression, serotonin-favoring amino-acid shifts), while elevated cortisol and possibly elevated prolactin reduce the system's tolerance for the transition. The result is disproportionate postprandial sleepiness.

RED-TEAM RULE
No mechanism may be treated as final unless competing explanations remain visible. For every major mechanism, the dossier must ask:
- Could this be epiphenomenal rather than causal?
- Could timing, sleep debt, or meal size explain more than the mechanism?
- Is the source direct primary human evidence or merely adjacent?

BOUNDARIES
This dossier is not diagnosing a disease. It is a mechanistic research synthesis. It also does not assume true reactive hypoglycemia unless documented at the time of symptoms.
